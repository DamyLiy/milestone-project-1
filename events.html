Vikings Event

The Viking Vengeance event is a bi-weekly alliance-based tower defense challenge where 20 waves of Viking NPCs attack your city. It is one of the best ways to earn Governor Gear materials. The goal isn't just to survive; it's to maximize "Defense Points" for both yourself and your alliance through coordination.

  1. The Wave Schedule
    • Waves 1-6:All alliance members are attacked.
    • Wave 7: Online members only.If you are offline, the Vikings skip you.
    • Waves 8-9:All alliance members.
    • Wave 10: The HQ (Headquarters) only.No individual cities are hit.
    • Waves 11-13:All alliance members.
    • Wave 14: Online members only.
    • Waves 15-16:All alliance members.
    • Wave 17: Online members only.
    • Waves 18-19:All alliance members.
    • Wave 20: The HQ only.Final boss wave.
  2. The "Empty City" Strategy (Pro Meta)
    • Step 1:Send ALL your troops (specifically Infantry and Cavalry) out to reinforce 2-3 different alliance members.
    • Step 2:Keep your top 3 strongest heroes at home. They will lead the reinforcements sent to you by your allies.
    • Step 3:Have allies reinforce you. When their troops kill Vikings in your city, they get high points, and you get "base" points for the defense.
    • Step 4: Leave Archers at home.Archers often "steal" kills from your reinforcements without providing much score. If you must send them, send them to the HQ.
  3. Role of Heroes in Vikings
    • Barricade Heroes (At Home):Keep your highest-level defensive/stat heroes here in home town. Their passive buffs apply to the reinforcements helping you.
    • Reinforcing Heroes (Sending Out):se high-lethality heroes (e.g., Chenko, Margot) to lead the troops you send to allies so they kill Vikings faster.
    • HQ Lead:For Waves 10 and 20, the alliance's strongest player should lead the HQ with a tank like Howard, Zoe and Alcar to ensure the entire alliance gets the completion bonus.

Bear Trap

To maximize your score in the Bear Hunt (Bear Trap), focus on Lethality and Attack. Since the bear does not counter-attack, defensive stats are useless.

  1. Best Heroes to Lead (Captain)
    • Heavy Attack heroesUse tier SSS heavy damage skills heroes such as Amadeus, Marlin, Petra, Hilda
    • F2P/Early:Helga (Lethality widget), Jabel, or Quinn.
  2. Best Heroes to Join
    • Chenko
    • Yeonwoo
    • Amadeus
    • Amane
    • Margot
  3. Optimized Troop Formation
    • The "Meta" Ratio: 10% Infantry / 10% Cavalry / 80% Archers.
