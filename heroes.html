Heroes' General Role

In Kingshot, heroes function as specialized leaders who determine your army's strength. Their roles are divided into three main categories:

  • Infantry (Tanks): High HP/Defense. Essential for the Front Row to absorb damage (e.g., Howard, Amadeus).
  • Archers (DPS): High damage, low health. Must stay in the Back Row (e.g., Quinn, Saul).
  • Cavalry (Flankers): Fast units that can bypass enemy tanks to hit archers (e.g., Chenko, Jabel).

Heroes

In Kingshot, heroes are categorized by their Generation (server age) and troop specialty. Here is the full list with their specific roles and descriptions.

  1. Generation 1 (Foundational)
    • Amadeus (Infantry - SSR): The "King of Offense" with the best Lethality buffs; remains top-tier for 5+ generations.
    • Jabel (Cavalry - SSR): The best F2P-friendly legendary for early rallies. She provides high offensive mobility.
    • Saul (Archer - SSR): The "Base Builder." His primary value is his Growth skill, which reduces construction time and costs.
    • Howard (Infantry - SR): The best purple tank. Essential for frontline defense in Arena and early PVE.
    • Chenko (Cavalry - SR): The "Rally Joiner." Her top-right skill boosts the Lethality of the entire group, making her mandatory for Bear Hunt events.
    • Diana (Archer - SR): The "Hunter." Her unique skill reduces the stamina cost of attacking monsters/beasts.
    • Quinn (Archer - SR): A solid backline damage dealer for Arena, though she is eventually outclassed by SSRs.
    • Gordon (Infantry - SR): A defensive specialist who excels at healing and holding captured buildings (Garrison).
    • Amane, Fahd, & Yeonwoo (SR): Late-Gen 1 additions; Amane and Yeonwoo serve as strong secondary rally joiners for extra Lethality.
  2. Generation 2 (meta Shift)
    • Zoe (Infantry - SSR): Replaces Howard. She is a defensive powerhouse with an AoE (Area of Effect) heal and damage skills, perfect for Garrison and Arena.
    • Marlin (Archer - SSR): A massive damage-per-second (DPS) boost. He has a 40% chance to significantly amplify squad damage every round.
    • Hilde (Cavalry - SSR): A heavy hitter for spenders. She applies a "Curse" that makes enemies take 50% more damage.
  3. Generation 3 (Controversial)
    • Eric (Infantry - SSR):The new standard for tanks. He focuses on extreme damage reduction and survivability
    • Petra (Cavalry - SSR):An offensive rally leader who rivals Hilde. She provides reliable Attack and Lethality buffs for Cavalry-heavy armies.
    • Jaeger (SSR - Archer):A strategic damage dealer who provides a long-term damage boost (3 rounds) but with a lower proc chance than Marlin.
  4. Generation 4
    • Rosa (SSR - Archer):The "Arena Queen." Her skill makes your entire team immune to debuffs (like stuns), completely changing PVP dynamics.
    • Margot (SSR - Cavalry):An offensive beast with massive AoE damage and high dodge capabilities.
    • Alcar (SSR - Infantry)Alcar has a more aggressive skill set. Pretty balanced hero for both garrison and rally calls
